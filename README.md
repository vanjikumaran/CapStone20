# WKF Karate Kata Selection and Gold Medal Outcomes

## Author

Vanjikumaran Sivajothy

---

## Executive Summary

This project explores the impact of Kata selection on winning gold medals in WKF-sanctioned karate tournaments. By analyzing scoring trends, strategic Kata choices, and competitor performance, we aim to uncover patterns that contribute to podium finishes.

---

## Rationale

Kata performance in WKF competitions is both technical and strategic. Athletes must choose the right Kata at the right time to maximize scores. Understanding which Kata are more likely to yield gold can inform athlete preparation, coaching strategies, and competition predictions.

---

## Research Question

**Does the choice of Kata significantly affect the likelihood of winning a gold medal in WKF competitions?**

---

## Data Sources

- Official WKF competition results (World Championships, Premier League events)
- Publicly available athlete performance data
- WKF scoring rulebooks for technical and athletic criteria

---

## Methodology

- **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**: Standardized Kata names, normalized score formats, removed incomplete matches.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Frequency distributions, score comparison, country and gender breakdowns.
- **Visualization Tools**: Bar charts, boxplots, heatmaps using Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib).
- **Statistical Correlation**: Analysis of score variances across Kata and medal outcomes.

---
